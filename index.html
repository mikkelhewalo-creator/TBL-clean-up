What the tool does

  • Keeps worksheets whose first row contains both “Mængde” and “Enhed”.
  • Deletes columns whose first-row header contains “Pris” or “Indeks”.
  • Swaps the Enhed and Mængde columns when Enhed is to the left.
  • Deletes qualifying empty or incomplete rows.
  • Removes cell background fills and conditional formatting.
  • Makes rows bold when column A has a value and columns B and C are blank.
  • Downloads the result as a new .xlsx file.

Your workbook is processed locally in your browser. It is not uploaded to this website or a server. For best preservation of formatting, use .xlsx files. Legacy .xls files are converted and may lose some formatting and workbook features.
